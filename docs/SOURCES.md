# Scientific source register

Research checked 2026-10-05. These sources support directions and approximate ranges, not the simulation's precise trajectory. Model coefficients marked illustrative are independently chosen educational assumptions. No model output is a clinical measurement or individual forecast.

| Topic | Source | What it supports | Implementation decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resting expenditure | [Mifflin et al., 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/) | A population equation relating resting expenditure to mass, height, age and sex. | Use the common rounded form; baseline 1668.75 kcal/day, with substantial individual uncertainty. |
| Dietary protein | [ISSN position stand, 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5477153/) | Approximately 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day for most exercising adults; exercise and protein support tissue synthesis. | Default 130 g/day; scenario adequacy reference 1.6 g/kg/day. Not a prescription or muscle construction requirement. |
| Glycogen | [Murray & Rosenbloom, 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6019055/), [Hearris et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5872716/) | Liver and skeletal muscle are different carbohydrate stores. Muscle uses its glycogen locally; exercise uses circulating and stored substrates. | Illustrative capacities 100 g liver and 400 g muscle, scaling with size. Do not imply these are universal limits. |
| Exercise expenditure | [2024 Adult Compendium: running](https://pacompendium.com/running/), [conditioning](https://pacompendium.com/conditioning-exercise/) | MET estimates vary with activity, intensity and rest periods; multi-exercise lifting and vigorous lifting differ. | Running estimate 10 MET; average resistance session 3.8 MET including rests. Net expenditure uses MET minus one. 3 km takes 18 min at the chosen 10 km/h. |
| Thermic effect | [Westerterp, 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC524030/) | Digesting and processing macronutrients requires energy; protein has the largest proportional thermic effect. | Select 25%, 7.5%, 2% of protein, carbohydrate and fat energy respectively. |
| Brain demand | [Raichle & Gusnard, 2002](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC124895/) | Large continuous brain demand; task-related changes are small relative to baseline. | Brain demand is already included in resting expenditure. Reading changes brain highlighting, with no added large calorie term. |
| Insulin | [Insulin signaling in health and disease, 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7773347/) | Tissue-specific signaling coordinates glucose uptake, glycogen storage and lipid handling. | Violet signal rings are separate from nutrient particles; dimensionless activity, no medical concentration. |
| Hypertrophy | [Resistance Training Variables for Optimization of Muscle Hypertrophy: An Umbrella Review, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9302196/) | Training dose, recovery and training context influence adaptation; muscle-specific loading matters. | A small bounded gain ceiling is an illustration. The review does not validate a three-year personal growth equation. |
| Cardiovascular adaptation | [Reimers et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6306777/) | Exercise can lower resting heart rate, with variability by mode and population. | Default 65 bpm tending toward 59 bpm is an illustrative parameterized curve. No quantitative stroke-volume prediction. |
| Body composition and accounting | [NIDDK Body Weight Planner](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/bwp), [Hall et al. model appendix](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/-/media/Files/BWP/Hall_Lancet_Web_Appendix.pdf) | Energy expenditure and body composition change dynamically; fixed calorie deficits do not translate into unlimited linear weight loss. | Update expenditure with mass; use illustrative stored energy densities 9400 kcal/kg fat and 1800 kcal/kg hydrated lean tissue. This is not a reimplementation of the NIH model. |

The schematic omits endocrine networks, lactate shuttling, renal glucose production, fluid shifts, individual variation, illness and many biochemical pathways. Amino acid signal intensity is not a gram-resolved whole-body nitrogen balance. Dietary lipid routing through lymph is shown schematically; biochemical colors are visual conventions.
