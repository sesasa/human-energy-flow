# Diet sources

Consulted 6 October 2026. The local extract is pinned to CoFID 2021 for reproducible calculations.

- [Public Health England / GOV.UK: CoFID 2021](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid) — food composition and original workbook.
- [CoFID user guide](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/60538e66d3bf7f03249bac58/McCance_and_Widdowsons_Composition_of_Foods_integrated_dataset_2021.pdf), pp. 6–7, 10–11, 15–16 — typical rather than definitive food values; per-100-g edible portions; trace/unknown conventions; monosaccharide equivalents at 3.75 kcal/g; distinct fibre conventions. The app adds measured AOAC fibre energy at 2 kcal/g consistently in its own model, and retains source kcal separately.
- NIH Office of Dietary Supplements: [iron](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-HealthProfessional/), [calcium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Calcium-HealthProfessional/), [magnesium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-HealthProfessional/), [potassium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Potassium-HealthProfessional/), [zinc](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/), [B12](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB12-HealthProfessional/), [folate](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Folate-HealthProfessional/), [vitamin C](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-HealthProfessional/), [vitamin D](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminD-HealthProfessional/), [omega-3](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Omega3FattyAcids-HealthProfessional/) — fixed adult male reference values, ALA distinguished from EPA/DHA, and food versus fortified folate.
- [EFSA summary of dietary reference values](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2017_09_DRVs_summary_report.pdf) — adult 250 mg/day EPA+DHA reference.
- [FDA: Sodium in Your Diet](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-education-resources-materials/sodium-your-diet) — 2,300 mg daily limit, not an adequacy goal.
- [Glycogen metabolism in athletes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6019055/) and [repeated sprint references](../revision-2/SOURCES.md) — directional link between carbohydrate availability, glycogen and exercise; not validation of this app’s numerical threshold or meal kernels.

The reference menu, kernel scales, food-dependent absorption multiplier, plate sizing, repeated-day/week initialization, 45% sprint-support threshold and long-term adaptation curves are implementation choices. Sources do not establish these as personal predictions. Micronutrient deficiency physiology is deliberately outside this model.
